# Independent admission and blinded grading plan

No reviewer has been assigned or represented as having performed review. This is a prospective procedure, not approval. Constructor identity is `codex_v7_trusted_constructor`; a model-produced review cannot satisfy the independent human requirement.

After the frozen generator creates twelve fault instances and two clean controls, automated admission must complete all252 clean/case/schedule/simulator executions. Any failed case prevents primary subjects. The trusted host may create an explicitly unapproved form with `tools/prepare_admission_review.py`; all human identity and decision fields start null.

An actual hardware-verification expert independent of fault construction must review all14 instances, source/parameter identities, private causal mechanisms, semantic overlap with exposed pilot and older study families, protected checker separation, all three schedules on all three simulators and frozen identifiability classes. Their record supplies actual identity/role, expertise, UTC date, independence declaration and artifact hashes. Declared independence is not cryptographic identity authentication. A class must be accepted exactly as frozen or the case rejected; it cannot be broadened after answers are seen. If novelty or identifiability is unestablished, reject/withhold approval. All-or-nothing admission means no partial primary cohort and no replacement/resampling within the frozen study.

The signed-off record remains private. It must explicitly approve every case and match actual execution evidence; missing decisions and constructor-written assertions do not authorize inference. Human review is required BEFORE primary captures/subjects, but not before constructing the protocol or generating frozen instances for review. No primary model run starts merely because automated admission passes.

After168 scheduled fresh subjects, an independent expert grades mode-blinded StageI submissions against the frozen rubric and private identifiability contract. Mode/repeat identifiers and StageII source/repair outcomes are withheld from the grading packet. Evidence content can make condition inference possible; report that residual blinding limitation rather than claiming perfect concealment. Mechanism/component conjunction is primary; repairs, intervention mechanical success and prediction discrimination are separate endpoints. Specific incorrect predictions, unknown grades, abstentions and failures remain explicit; unknown is not silently converted to zero. Disagreements preserve original scores and written resolution. No primary effect analysis runs until all scheduled outcomes and required human adjudications exist.

No tool is authorized to contact or publish to an outside reviewer without explicit user instructions. These materials are local review packets only. If independent review is unavailable, preserve the admitted frozen catalog and report primary inference NOT_RUN with that exact reason; do not substitute a synthetic reviewer or bypass the gate.
